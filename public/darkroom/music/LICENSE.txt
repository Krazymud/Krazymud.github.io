Darkroom reel radio — music licensing
========================================

Tracks in this folder MUST be public-domain recordings or works you own the
rights to distribute on the public web. Do not copy proprietary or CDN-hosted
audio from third-party gift sites (e.g. roll.giadaserena.top).

Current files (tone-a.mp3, tone-b.mp3, tone-c.mp3) are short synthetic
placeholder tones generated locally with ffmpeg for UI wiring and automated
tests only. They are labeled in the playlist as 「测试曲 A/B/C」.

Before production deploy, replace these placeholders with properly licensed
public-domain performances (see Musopen, IMSLP, or your own recordings) and
update src/darkroom/reel/playlist.ts titles accordingly.
